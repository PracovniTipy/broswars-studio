CASTLE DIMENSION - CREDITS AND LICENSES
============================================

Game design, code, music and sound effects: original work (audio is generated by code).
Original procedural art: terrain textures, UI frames, castles and coloured tower skins, world maps, particles and effects.

All third-party art below is used under the licenses stated by its authors. Where an asset is offered under several licenses, it is used under the Creative Commons / OGA-BY option.
Assets were modified for this game (cropping, scaling, recolouring, compositing into characters and sprite atlases). Modified versions of CC-BY-SA assets are made available under the same CC-BY-SA license.
